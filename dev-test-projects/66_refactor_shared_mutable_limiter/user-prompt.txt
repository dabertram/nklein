Give the rate limiter in src/ its own state, and keep every existing caller working.

Its counters, settings and clock are module-level bindings, so two limiters cannot exist at once and no test can
isolate itself. The limiter also records denials through the audit log while the audit log reads the limiter's
configuration back, so neither module can be loaded alone.

Read specification.md for the goals and the rules. Note the constraint that makes this interesting: the
module-level convenience functions must keep working exactly as they do now. Removing shared state while keeping
the shared entry points is the exercise.
