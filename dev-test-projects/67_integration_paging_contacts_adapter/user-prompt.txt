Write the client adapter in src/adapter.mjs for the contacts service shipped in service/.

The service is deterministic and in-process, and it never throws: every call comes back as a status and a body,
including the failures. Turning those into outcomes an application can use — a list that pages, a missing record
that is an answer rather than an error, a validation failure that names its fields — is the whole job.

Read specification.md for the contract and the rules. One thing worth repeating: the service records every call
you make, and some conformance cases check HOW you talked to it, not just what you returned. Fetching everything
and filtering locally will not pass.
