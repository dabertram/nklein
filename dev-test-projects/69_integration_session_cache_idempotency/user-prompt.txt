Write the inventory client in src/adapter.mjs.

The service needs a bearer token that expires, it answers a repeated read with 304 when you send back the etag it
gave you, and it requires an idempotency key on writes. So the client has to hold a session, keep a small cache,
and mint keys carefully.

Read specification.md for the contract. Most of what is graded here is what you SEND, not what you return: the
conformance suite reads the service's call log. A retry that mints a fresh idempotency key looks fine from the
outside and quietly creates duplicates, and that case exists precisely to catch it.

Time is injected. Use the now() and sleep() you are given; a real timer cannot move this service's clock.
