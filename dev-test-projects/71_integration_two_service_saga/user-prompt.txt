Write the order coordinator in src/adapter.mjs.

Placing an order touches two services that share no transaction: money is held on the ledger, goods are booked on
fulfilment, and only then is the money captured. When the middle step fails, the hold has to be released — and
when the last step's outcome is unknown, it must NOT be released, because a timeout is not a "no" and refunding
money the ledger has already captured is worse than the original failure.

Read specification.md for the contract. Every case checks what both services were left holding, not just what
placeOrder returned: a coordinator that returns the right error and leaves a dangling hold has failed at the one
thing it exists to do.
