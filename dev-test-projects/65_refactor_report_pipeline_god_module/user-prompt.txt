Break the expense reporting in src/ into stages that can be read and tested on their own, without changing a
single character of its output.

One function splits lines, applies five rejection rules, groups by category, computes averages, pads a table and
totals it. The money formatter is copied verbatim into the receipts module, so any rounding change makes the two
disagree.

Read specification.md for the goals and the rules. Be careful with the output: column widths, padding, rejection
wording and the ORDER rejections are reported in are all observable, and all of them are pinned by frozen
scenarios.
